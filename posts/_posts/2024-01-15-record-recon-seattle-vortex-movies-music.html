Another record store review. This one was the first recon of mission 1 from this endeavor detailed a few months ago here.

Vortex Music & Movies, located at 12439 116th Ave NE in Kirkland, Washington, is a store that specializes in buying and selling quality LPs, Blu-Ray, DVDs, and CDs. They have a vast collection of thousands of collectible-quality items in stock. The store is known for constantly adding new and rare items to their inventory, catering to a wide range of music and movie enthusiasts.
