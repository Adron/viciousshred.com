Tucked away in the cozy enclave of Millvale, just a stone’s throw away from Pittsburgh, lies a veritable treasure trove for audiophiles and vinyl enthusiasts: Attic Records. As one steps inside, there’s an immediate sense of stepping back in time, a throwback to an era when records weren’t just a way to listen to music, but a holistic experience.

Attic Records boasts an impressive range of records spanning various genres and eras. From classic rock to soul, jazz to blues, and pop to folk, there’s a record for every music lover out there. The staff, knowledgeable and friendly, are always willing to guide you through the maze of vinyl, whether you’re a seasoned collector or a newcomer just dipping your toes into the world of records. The store itself exudes an old-world charm with wooden racks filled with both new arrivals and old classics, lending to an ambiance that’s both nostalgic and inviting.

Heavy Metal

For heavy metal aficionados, Attic Records doesn’t disappoint. The heavy metal selection is nothing short of splendid. They house a variety of classic bands, from Metallica to Iron Maiden, Judas Priest to Black Sabbath, alongside a plethora of underground gems waiting to be discovered. Whether you’re after the pioneering albums of the 70s and 80s, or the more contemporary hits from the metal sub-genres of the 90s and 2000s, the chances are that Attic Records has what you’re craving. Vinyl collectors will appreciate the quality of the pressings, many in mint or near-mint condition, ensuring a listening experience that’s as pure as it gets.

Attic Records is not just a store; it’s a journey into the world of music. The vast selection, coupled with the expertise of the staff, ensures that every visit is a memorable one. For those in and around Pittsburgh, it’s a must-visit, and for visitors to the city, it’s worth the detour to Millvale to explore this musical haven.

Consider Attic Records a top stop if you’re ever in the area, and if you live in the Pittsburgh metro just go ahead and add Attic Records to your weekly perusal! For a walk through of my visit, check out the segment in my VLOG.
