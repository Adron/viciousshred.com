As the darkness confines me to my corner this night
A lacona coiled sings angelically to kill the light

The interspliced metal strings resonate the emotions
to lift up a notion implied by written devotions
To process to compile
A love I do not revile

The draw is persistent
A lambda is consistent

No contradiction lies
Lacking abstract ties

Build, fail, test, fail, fast, fail, learn, fail.
Ideas consistent, failure fleeting, victory.

NOTE: Probably the nerdiest lyrical tryst I’ve written, maybe ever. I’d be high impressed if someone knew the two key things I’m writing about with these lyrics. Unless you are a coder, then I’ll be maybe moderately impressed.
