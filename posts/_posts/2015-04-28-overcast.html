What do you see and what do you know.
It’s overcast and I’m opposite of low.

I received your smile I didn’t expect.
In turn my day changed on that effect.

Chin up and a smiling grin I marched.
Meetings to strike forward and demarche.

Sky leads me thinking forward
clouds I’m below and walk toward

Overcast, seen in kind.
Overcast, eyes do find.

I ponder tomorrow
with little sorrow
waiting for the time
to step out of line

Overcast, seen in kind.
Overcast, eyes do find.

An overcast sky above
with firm earth below
stepping into unknowns.

inspired by affection
simple gestures alone
I work to make known.

A story I want for us
to see into your eyes
to know I’m not alone.

Overcast, seen in kind.
Overcast, eyes do find.

never go away, just come some day,
here near lay, put your hand in mine,
find some time, we’ll see in kind.

Overcast, seen in kind.
Overcast, eyes do find.
