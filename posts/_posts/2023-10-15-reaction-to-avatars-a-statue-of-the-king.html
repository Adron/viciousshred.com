A comment on the video stood out with some great extra details, check out what “@Ginger_Monster” wrote.

They are very consistent in their work. It was the very first clip of the King era, but at the same time it is the epilogue of their featurette Legend of Avatar Country: A Metal Odyssey, which was filmed November, 21 – December, 20 2018 (for 24 days in 12-19 hour HEAVY work days, 1 day-off). Рost-production finished October 6 2019. Released at their official site November, 22 2019, put in Youtube March, 5 2020.
A Statue of the King shot back to back with The King Wants You in 2017. In The King Wants You was father, mother and son, in Statue only father and son.😢
Hail the Apocalypse is the reference to same video.😄 The girl from crowd will become the guard in Dance Devil Dance (2022).😁 Helicopter pilot is their make up artist Emma Falkensjö, work with them since Smells like a Freakshow video and still.🤗

Speaking about royalty waving… Seventh album is based on the inner joke of the band that Jonas is the king. In 2011, Jonas changed his Facebook name to Kungen (Кing in Swedish) and told them to address him that way. Such a time-killing humor on the road – he enters, waving his hand royally like the Queen of Britain, everyone in the band praises him. Or he gave everyone signed photos of himself. 😂😂😂Yep, this small detail have a long story.
