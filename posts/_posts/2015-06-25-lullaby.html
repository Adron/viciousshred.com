by: Lenadron

Dear sunshine passing by…
Darkening clouds fill the sky..

Pretty angel close your eyes..
See the bird that sings and flies..

See the magic world of yours…
There you’ve power, strengths and force..

There is nothing you should hide..
Guards protect you by your side..

Here you make your own rules..
Peace and goodness, love and truth..

What you wish to bird you say..
It will bring it to your way..

Little angel close your eyes..
Now your dream adventure starts..

Today has lost it fire
I can see you tire

So close those pretty eyes
For today just say goodbye

I’ll tuck you in goodnight
rest that weary mind
know I think of you tonight
slumber you will find
