By: Me

sometimes people talk to much.
sometimes they don’t see enough.

almost always people don’t hear.
almost always they miss reality.

perception is a hard thing to know.
perception is a great thing to feel.

feel to know and know to feel reality
but know feelings aren’t perpetuity
to decide to feel to spite to know
it’s a sure fire way to know anguish
it’s a sure fire way to dissilusion
it’s a fire sure way to writhe in pain

Blood eagle screams, no pain close, nothing near.
Blood eagle screams, greatest slain, only pain.

Reality is torn from the feelings, but we act altogether knowing,
albeit blind from visions reeling, do we know our own mind knowing?

…so much more to this one, as the knowledge pervades and the feelings become a wrecked day.
