The song “Moon Over Paradise” starts off almost like it’s going to be a ballad with a little acoustic effects based riff, but within seconds shifts into heavy hitting rock. I dig it!

As I watched the video, which is filmed in a graffiti laden old building, with lit candles, and other things that alludes to a darkened atmosphere which is barely lit. It’s a wicked good visage in which to watch the song that Natasha brings to us, as it unfolds with her amazing soulful, powerful, and smart vocals.

As she touts, leading toward another powerful chorus, she didn’t take her medicine. I can only think that if this is the result, toss that medicine and keep making music like this! Powerful, from the heart, hitting, and absolutely beautifully put together song.

As Natasha delivers this absolutely banger of a song, my ears are perked up the whole time as the bass, drums, and guitar all fit the song really well. Whoever she worked with, as I’ve not uncovered all the details yet, have meshed flawlessly!

I’ll be keeping an eye out, and looking for the upcoming album of Natasha’s. Consider me officially a fan! 🤘🏻
