A bit mistaken humanity
it’s all steeped in blue
not the infalability
not the deep red rue.

Devil’s fire sparks a dare
the hottest flame is blue
innocent the soul goes fare
no weakest flame of red rue.

Passion beats the heart
blood courses through veins
again the life is lit blue
not deathly bled red rue.

Burning blue, coursing blue…
