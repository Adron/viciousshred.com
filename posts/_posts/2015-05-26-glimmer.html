A long while ago distant to today
a friend has fallen laid to rest
permanently life was ripped away
all while just enjoying the best.

As the flange slides and wheels roll
the horizon screams silence in black
working dilligently a passionate toll
tonight the darkness empty does lack.

I want to reach out
see your eyes glimmer again
hear the music about
to hear the angel’s song

Frayed sanity seems to dwindle away
the mind wraps around new candy
but what to say as the stab today
today just a corpse sadly laid sandy

hear the music about
to hear the angel’s song

Goodbye my friend I never knew
a fellow soul striving like few
stand aside fickle rotton hue
you’re laid to rest in love too.
