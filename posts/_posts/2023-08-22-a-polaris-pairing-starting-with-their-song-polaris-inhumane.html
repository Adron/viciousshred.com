This song starts off with such a great grungy bass line that then goes into – not a grungy song – but a tight, heavy, unrelenting song with a slight touch of groove on top! This is a stellar song, with an amazing band that has brought together a great sound, punching bass, melodic, harmonic, and rhythmic guitars that tread into new territories. All that with a very minor spice of electronic overtones here and there. Enjoy!
