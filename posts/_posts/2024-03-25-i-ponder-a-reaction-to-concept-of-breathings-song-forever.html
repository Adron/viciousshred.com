“Forever” by Concept of Breath is a track that sweeps you into an auditory landscape so rich and distinct, it’s as if you’re discovering music for the first time. The esoteric guitars weave a tapestry of sound that’s both complex and inviting, creating a backdrop that’s at once familiar and utterly foreign. These are not just strings being plucked or strummed; they’re whispers from another dimension, guiding you through the track’s nuanced journey. The melody they craft feels like a secret being told in hushed tones, one that you’re privileged to overhear.

Then there’s the vocal sound – an oddball concoction of timbres that might initially catch you off guard, but soon becomes a very compelling part of the experience. The vocalist doesn’t just sing; it twists, turns, and dances around the melody, sometimes aligning with the guitars, at other times diverging to explore its own peculiar path. It’s this vocal sound that imbues the song with a sense of mystique and individuality. The singer manages to convey emotion not just through the lyrics, but through the very texture and color of their voice, which is no small feat.

A band to watch, and a song to listen to more than once. Give it a go. The band and song gets a thumbs up for me.
