The sun shines blindingly bright
but the darkness brings visible light

It seems a beauty in white, a dove
graces the soul today in love.

A racing thought, innocently bound
to a passion to and desire found

Crest upon your singing lips
the silence opens forth and rips

My soul, my mind, my heart and all
Is all yours as I do happily fall
