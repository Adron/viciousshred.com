Some lyrics that have popped into mind while listening to Illumishade’s new album. Pondering the past alone, as one in my position would do.

Would you, let me know when the good ole' times are in play
cuz it seems we don't always know when we're in their midst.
The exuberance of the turbulence of it all just day to day
they only give a wink and a nod at their timing, fleeting fits.

I want to remember the days, but here I'm alone
I want to remember the days, but friends lie in six
I want to gaze upon the days' memories.
I want to look upon the eyes decadently.
I want to think of the times as if yesterday
I want to know they where life well lived
I want to stand up and be able to see today
as a well lived time that deeply bows to yesterday.
