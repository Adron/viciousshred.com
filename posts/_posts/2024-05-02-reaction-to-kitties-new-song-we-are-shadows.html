For those who have been orbiting the metal scene since the late ’90s, Kittie isn’t just a band; they’re a beacon of raw, feminine power in a genre traditionally dominated by male performers. Emerging from Ontario, Canada in 1996, the band was formed by sisters Mercedes and Morgan Lander. Their debut album, Spit, released in 1999, was a combustible mix of aggressive riffs and angsty lyrics that quickly resonated with a generation of fans eager for a fresh, fierce voice. This wasn’t just another act; this was a full-frontal assault on the senses, and it carved out a niche.

Fast forward to today, and Kittie has unleashed their latest single “We Are Shadows.” This track stands as a potent reminder of the band’s enduring legacy and their ability to evolve while staying true to their core. From the opening riff, it’s clear that “We Are Shadows” is both a nod to their roots and a step into the deeper, shadowy waters of their musical journey. Morgan Lander’s vocals, ever so piercing and commanding, weave through the melody, holding a perfect balance between melody and maelstrom.

Lyrically, the song explores themes of resilience and the hidden strengths that emerge in times of darkness. This isn’t just about personal battles; it’s a collective anthem that resonates with the shared struggles of their audience. The guitar work is meticulous, layering gritty textures over pounding drums that drive the track forward. It’s a song that doesn’t just aim to be heard; it demands it, echoing the spirit Kittie has embodied since their inception.
