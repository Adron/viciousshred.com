Hand of Juno’s latest offering, ‘We’ve Built the Line,’ is a smorgasbord of esoteric charm and industrial grit, wrapped in an aesthetic that’s as intriguing as the music itself. The video is a visual feast, dominated by leather and avant-garde outfits that seem to scream rebellion with every stitch. The band members are decked out in an array of leather ensembles that range from dystopian chic to edgy punk, each outfit seemingly tailored to enhance their individual musical personas.

A standout moment in the video is the bass player, whose name I unfortunately don’t have, commanding attention not just with her attire but with her instrument’s deep, smacklicious thump. She’s draped in a sleek leather outfit that perfectly encapsulates the blend of power and allure, embodying the spirit of the song. Her presence is magnetic, and when she sexily drops those bass lines, it’s more than just a musical moment; it’s a statement.

The bass itself is a character in its own right, with a sound so rich and textured it feels like an auditory pulse driving the song forward. It adds a weighty, sensual undercurrent to the tune, perfectly complementing the industrial overtones and the visually striking leather aesthetics of the video.

‘We’ve Built the Line’ is not just a song; it’s an experience. Hand of Juno has managed to create a piece that’s as much about visual storytelling as it is about auditory excellence. The leather-laden visuals and the thumping bass lines come together to create a piece that’s edgy, esoteric, and utterly captivating.
