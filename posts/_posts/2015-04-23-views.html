I stand looking out at the city pondering emotions that run deep. Inspiration has lit my soul afire and my mind awake like they’ve not been for too long. I’m enjoying this feeling as I step forward eyes peeled looking for new ways. What’s next, what do I do, how will I do what I will need to achieve what my aim is.

Ahh. The gasp I have leaves my chest as I tense from the intensity of thought. I sit and look forward, with as recently told to me, the fire in my eyes. The blue eyed devil, it seems I’ve always been, can’t deny what I’ve been given. The night is wonderful to me, as my eyes can truly see, opened up and at ease. The glimmer of lights, so many more, lose their plight against the darkness as if it were a surrounding sea.

Views above, to the ground below, I watch the world tick tock forward.
