In the realm of progressive metal, a new anthem has emerged – or endeavored to – that resonates with the echoes of the genre’s golden era while firmly planting its roots in the modern soil. This is “Chaos Master” by Jelusick, a song that not only showcases the band’s musical prowess but also serves as a bridge between the past and the present of glam metal – or endeavored to.

Lyrical Journey of ‘Chaos Master’

The song itself is a narrative of turmoil and resilience. It delves into themes of inner conflict and the chaos that often reigns in our minds. The lyrics are a poignant reflection of the struggle to find order in the midst of life’s tumultuous journey. Jelusick doesn’t just sing about chaos; they encapsulate the feeling of being in the eye of the storm, offering a cathartic experience for the listener.

All that written, I’ll add, if this is your type of song. It could land very flat and the band overall, albeit extremely proficient, don’t stand out in any particular way among the many bands out there at this time. They have some unique characteristics, but they’re often overshadowed by the general age of the genre they bring to use with Chaos Master. Even though there is turmoil and resilience, I could have easily missed this song if I weren’t paying attention via a reaction video.

A Nod to Progressive Glam Metal

“Chaos Master” does have a modern sound and throws in a nostalgic essence of progressive glam metal. The song pays homage to the heyday of the genre, reminiscent of the grandeur of bands like Queen and the theatricality of Alice Cooper. Yet, they don’t just dwell in the past, Jelusick infuses contemporary elements, making the song more relevant to today’s audience. It’s a balancing act that they execute, appealing to both old-school aficionados and new listeners – for those ready to put in the time to enjoy the song.

Conclusion

In conclusion, “Chaos Master” by Jelusick is a good addition to the progressive metal landscape. It’s a song that respects its roots while forging its own path. For fans of the genre, it’s a reminder of why they fell in love with glam-ish progressive-ish metal in the first place. For for newcomers, it’s an introduction to a world where chaos and melody coexist in a beautiful symphony. Overall I give the song a solid 6 of 10 and the video a solid 6.5 of 10. Not bad, but not yet in the realm of greatness.
