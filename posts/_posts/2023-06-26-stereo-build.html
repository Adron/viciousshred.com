Today I kick off a series of videos and content I put together around a home stereo project. Considering how things are these days, one often just buys a pre-packaged whatever – computer, TV – and those things might have nicer or upgraded speakers and that’s what music is played through.

However, I’m a little bit more into music than that, so I took the next step and returned to an existence where I’ve not got a proper full range hi fidelity home stereo system. The following are the details, links to further details, and videos of the unboxing, assembly, configuration, and first record play.

The first VLOG is a short one, coming in at a mere minute and fifty seconds. I introduce what I’m up to and get started with basic setup for the stereo system.
