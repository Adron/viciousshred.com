UPDATE March 19th 2024: Just dropped a reaction vid to the latest from this upcoming album Phantoma for Ghost in the Mist, check that out with me too!

“Unleash the Archers’ latest track, ‘Green & Glass,’ is a masterful blend of power metal dynamics and melodic intricacies that showcases the band’s continued evolution and prowess in the metal scene. From the first note, the song grabs listeners with its compelling guitar riffs, thunderous drumming, and the unparalleled vocal range of frontwoman Brittney Slayes. The song’s composition is a journey through epic landscapes, seamlessly weaving together moments of intense speed and power with passages of melodic introspection.
