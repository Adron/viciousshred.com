“Human Becoming” by Calva Louise is an exhilarating journey through sound, an audacious leap into the uncharted territories of musical composition that defies conventional boundaries and expectations. From the first note, it’s clear that this isn’t just another album; it’s a manifesto of innovation, a declaration of artistic freedom that resonates with the bold spirit of its creators.

What sets “Human Becoming” apart is how it eschews the standard verse-chorus mold, opting instead for a more fluid and dynamic structure that captivates and surprises at every turn. This isn’t music that you merely listen to; it’s an experience that engulfs you, demanding your full attention and rewarding it with layers of complexity and emotion that unfold with each listen.

The stylistic choices made throughout the album are nothing short of visionary. Tracks seamlessly blend elements of punk, electronic, and psychedelic rock, creating a soundscape that is both eclectic and cohesive. The use of synths and guitars is particularly noteworthy, weaving together melodies and riffs that are at once familiar and utterly unique. The vocals, too, are a highlight, ranging from ethereal whispers to powerful crescendos that convey a spectrum of human emotions and experiences.

Following the algorithm to “Human Becoming” feels like stumbling upon a hidden gem, a reminder of the magic that happens when artists dare to venture beyond the conventional and into the realm of the extraordinary. Calva Louise has not just created an album; they’ve crafted a masterpiece that challenges and expands the listener’s understanding of what music can be.

As a fan, the excitement of experiencing “Human Becoming” is akin to witnessing the unveiling of a masterpiece. It’s a thrilling reminder of the endless possibilities that lie in the pursuit of creative expression. This album is a beacon for those yearning for something different, something that doesn’t just conform to the mainstream but reshapes it in its own image.
