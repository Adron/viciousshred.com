Friday, April 17, 2015 – one of those weird pieces I’ve written more as fore-shadowing to future things I see versus anything that is real or is happening, but is likely to happen. Oh for foreshadowing adds such complexity to the vision with the riddle of life.

There you are thousands of miles away and I don’t know what to do
as you enrapture me and everything that I know about you.

Your ideas, passions, and musical love is so intriguing
I’m glad to know you and even to spend what I can of my being.

Everything about the way you stand out even amidst your qualms and pains
I’ve seen in our short time so much and I know that I love your aims

I’d do everything that I could to encourage and guide your way
just because of the things you’ve given me in this day.

It seems a sea to stand between us but I hope to call you friend
and hope to see you step beyond barriers and standing beyond the ends.

Your fellow musical soul binds us and so much more inside our minds
I wonder what else we could step toward with treasure and other finds.

Again all I can say is you’re an amazing soul
I’d do what I can and stand beside your side
To see you stand above it all proud strong and bona fide

So much more to say
and never seems to fade away
would be better a never ending way
to step forward and see another day

See your eyes and smile
Gets me and gets me riled
To take the day for mine
And take what I can find
I’ve handed all to you
And all the things to few
Just to see you stand above it all with a smile…
