“Prisoner” by Novelist is a song that delves into the themes of feeling trapped and searching for freedom. The lyrics seem to explore the struggles of being confined, whether by physical, emotional, or societal constraints. The artist uses some vivid storytelling and metaphors to express a sense of yearning for escape and the pain of being held back.
