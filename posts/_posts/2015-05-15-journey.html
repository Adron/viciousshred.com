I look upon the sky ever so free.
you bring unknown feelings to me.

my mind goes on sailing this way.
the excitement enraptures today.

a distant place a journey to make.
great lights and a place we take.

it’ll be ours, others best clear.
it’s ours to take, a moment there.

it’ll be ours, no others there.
it’s ours to take, it’s here clear.

enraptured my excitement builds.
I feel I’ll need to run afield.

My heart beats so rapidly I reel.
grinning, knowing excitement I feel.
