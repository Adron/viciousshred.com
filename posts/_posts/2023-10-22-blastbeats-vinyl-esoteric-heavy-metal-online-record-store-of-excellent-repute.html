Each record tells a story, a symphony of metal history waiting to be played. From the obscure to the iconic, Blastbeats Vinyl has it all. And who is this for, you ask? It’s for the true metal heads, those who can discern the nuances of a guitar riff, those who live and breathe the raw energy of metal.

Now, I (Adron Hall) have traversed the vinyl landscape, placing orders from countless establishments. Yet, out of the myriad of orders, Blastbeats Vinyl stands out, not just for their impeccable collection but for their unwavering reliability and consistency in getting orders shipped and received in perfect condition. Time and again, they’ve proven to be the most consistent, the most trustworthy. To add icing to the cake, they’ve delivered with a little extra multiple times, such as stickers and the like.

In a world where consistency is a rarity, Blastbeats Vinyl is a testament among the many. So, fellow metal heads, if you’re on a quest for esoteric, but exquisite record selections, you know where to look.

Go check them out @ https://blastbeatsvinyl.com/
