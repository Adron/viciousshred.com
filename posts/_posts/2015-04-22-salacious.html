The patriarchy screams to have monogamy
while the soul longs for sexual ecstasy

The turpid confines tortues the mind
while the smile brings a salacious find

I see. I touch. I want. I love.
I can’t forever be, you see only free
To have, to want, to know, to touch…

A salacious mind, forbidden lustful kind,
turned you look behind, I feel my breath bind,
Arms are intertwined, our bodies combined.

These dreams have me prurient
your beauty very libidinous
lust for you wonderfully variant

The world is split into fractals,
the heart beats seemingly fragile.

Her hair brings more dreams…
her gaze passion teems…
I see her eyes open so wide…
to know her mind confide…

I’ll take you if you take me…
one time is it all that we need.
It’s as if we saw this…
the first time we looked forthwith.

turned you look behind, I feel my breath bind,

Don’t second guess this know it’s right…

Ride down on me I’ll take you tightly…
breath this deep pleasure rightly.
My fingers running through your hair…
you back arching debonaire.
