Devourer, a band shrouded in mystery and darkness, emerges from the depths of the underground music scene decades along with their latest release, “Wicked Ones.” Comprised of lead vocalistk, lyricist, and bassist Oksana Falk. On guitars, John Falk. Devourer is a force to be reckoned within the realm of dark metal.

The music video single ‘The Wicked Ones’ was taken from our upcoming new album. The new album is the fifth full-length album in the band’s two-decades-long history and will be released in 2024. Oksana Falk played the bass guitar on the previous Devourer album ‘Raptus’ released in 2022, but after Fredrik Håf left the band in 2023, she’s now also the vocalist of Devourer. The last three albums were released by Iron, Blood & Death Corporation, but Devourer is not signed to any label.
