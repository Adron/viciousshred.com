Because Atlas isn’t ready to hold the world.
He shall, but until then I’ll be here.

Fight on today.
Fight tomorrow.

Strive through it all blood strewn
Fight through it all pain in hand.
Walk, trample, stumble, or flown
Mind the matter ignore pain and stand.

Try.
Fight.
Endeavor.
Try.
Fight.
Stand for Atlas.
Stand for the world.
Stand for Atlas.
Stand, even in spite of it all.
