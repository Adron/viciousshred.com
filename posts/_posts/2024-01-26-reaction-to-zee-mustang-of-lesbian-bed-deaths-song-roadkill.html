Lesbian Bed Death’s latest track ‘Roadkill’ hits the scene with all the subtlety of a neon-painted hearse in a funeral procession. If you’re into a brand of rock that sounds like it was brewed in a cauldron of gothic clichés and sprinkled with a dash of punk rebellion, then this might just be your jam.

The song trudges along with guitar riffs that are as predictable as a horror movie plot, and lyrics that seem to have been dug up from the graveyard of teenage angst. The band does show some skill, clinging to their rock roots like a lifeline, but the overall effect is akin to wearing sunglasses at night – trying a bit too hard to be cool. It’s clear they’re having a blast, and there’s a certain charm to their commitment to the goth-punk aesthetic, but let’s just say it’s an acquired taste. ‘Roadkill’ is okay rock ‘n’ roll – it’s got the right ingredients, but it’s like ordering a cocktail only to realize you prefer beer. Not exactly my cup of tea, but hey, to each their own brand of musical necromancy.
